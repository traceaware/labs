How to read this map

Every organisation in the AWARE network appears once. The Final Brands sit in the top row. Everyone else sits below, in the row that matches how many connections away they are from the nearest Final Brand.

The rows: Final Brands and steps

Final Brands
The top row. These are the brands whose products the supply chain ends with.
Step 1
Connected directly to a Final Brand.
Step 2
Connected to a Step 1 organisation, and not directly to any brand.
Step 3
Connected to a Step 2 organisation. This is the furthest the network reaches today.

A step counts recorded connections. It measures distance from a brand and says nothing about what the organisation makes. An organisation connected to several brands sits at the step of the nearest one. In this data, no two Final Brands connect directly. They are linked through shared suppliers.

Trades without a connection

Two organisations have moved tokens to each other, and the connections data has no accepted connection between them. A solid line needs a recorded connection, so the map draws a dashed line instead. There are such pairs.

Where this comes from:

  • A connection is missing from the export. The export keeps one route per organisation per brand and leaves out connections between organisations it has already found. A real connection can drop out this way. This is the most likely cause.
  • The organisations traded without ever connecting. Tokens can move between organisations that never formally connected.

Dashed lines do not count towards steps. Steps come from solid connections only. To see a pair, select one organisation: its panel lists each trading partner under "Trades without a connection", with the tokens and transfers involved. Switch the dashed lines off with the "Trades without a connection" toggle in the sidebar.

When you select an organisation

  • Route to a Final Brand. The shortest chain of connections from the organisation to a brand, drawn in red on the map.
  • Closer to a Final Brand. Connections one step nearer to a brand.
  • Same step. Connections to organisations in the same row.
  • Further away. Connections one step further from a brand.
  • Tokens moved. The total tokens in the organisation's transfers.

Controls

  • Find an organisation. Search by name or Aware ID. Enter jumps to the first match.
  • Follow one brand. Highlights every organisation reachable from that brand without passing through another Final Brand. Treat the count as a minimum.
  • Country. Fades organisations in other countries.
  • Show. Switch roles or the dashed lines on and off.
  • Move around. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, Fit to see everything, Esc to clear the selection.

What the data leaves out

  • Some real connections are missing, for the reason above.
  • Demo and test accounts are removed. Possible duplicate organisations are flagged and not merged.
  • Steps are measured from all Final Brands at once.

About this data

Built from the latest sync, with known repairs applied. Possible duplicate organisations are flagged and not merged.

The export keeps one discovery route per organisation per brand, so a few real connections are missing. Dashed lines show organisations that exchange tokens with no connection recorded.

Following one brand blocks every other Final Brand as a pass-through, so it shows that brand's reach as a minimum.
