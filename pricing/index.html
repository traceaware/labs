Two roles, one platform. Whether you make materials or manage them, you pay only for what you produce - EU compliance and blockchain-secured proof included.

What do you produce?

Producer
You make materials or products.
You need to prove what you made, how you made it, and what impact it had.
Raw material suppliers Fibre & yarn producers Fabric manufacturers Product manufacturers
Product owner
You receive goods and report on them.
You need to verify where your materials came from and meet buyer and regulatory requirements.
Product manufacturers Trading companies Wholesalers Brands & retailers

Product manufacturers often need both - a producer membership to secure their own batches, and a product owner membership to receive and report on incoming materials.

Good to know

Membership feeOne-time upfront annual payment. First year: €650. From year two: €1,150.
Data tokensBilled monthly on a yearly commitment. 1 token = 1 kg of verified material.
Free entry tierAvailable to producers only, up to 0.25M kg/year. Membership fee still applies.
Token validityFree tokens valid 12 months. Purchased tokens valid 24 months. Unused tokens expire.
Product manufacturersMay need both memberships - producer and product owner - each billed separately.
CurrencyAll prices in EUR. Where agreed with The Movement B.V. directly, governed by Dutch law.
